Who this site is for

PelvisPower is for adults. You must be 18 or older, or the age of majority where you live if that is higher, to register and read.

What you will find here

Frank, friendly exercise courses for the muscles behind sexual pleasure and sensation, with plain talk about bodies. It is educational. It contains no pornographic images or video, and nothing involving minors. You can stop at any time and skip anything you do not want to do.

Not medical or therapeutic advice

PelvisPower and Dress for Caress are education and personal exploration. They are not medical care, physiotherapy, therapy or a substitute for any of them. If you have pain, bleeding, a medical condition, a recent surgery, a pregnancy or a recent birth, ask a qualified clinician before you try an exercise. Stop if anything hurts.

Your access

Access is free after you register, until the date shown on the site. Content is for your own personal use. Do not copy, resell or publish it, or share it as your own.

Your information

When you register we keep your email address, the time you agreed to these terms, your confirmation that you are 18 or older and that you understand the nature of the material, and the language you used. We use your email to remember that you registered and to send you a welcome message and news about the courses. We do not sell or share your information.

We use only the cookie needed to remember your registration. Our service providers include Cloudflare (hosting and storage) and Resend (email delivery).

To see or delete your information, write to hello@pelvispower.org.
