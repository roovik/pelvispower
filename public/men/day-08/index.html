The Rock

Hips that move

Men spend an astonishing amount of life sitting. Chairs, cars and couches leave the hips stiff and the floor sleepy. Today you wake up the whole region with gentle movement.

The Rock is a pelvic tilt. Lie on your back with your knees bent. Tilt your pelvis so your lower back presses toward the floor, then roll the other way so your back arches a little.

Rocking back and forth stretches the lower back and massages the muscles around your floor. Add slow hip circles for good measure. Many men feel the lower back ease and the whole pelvis come alive.

Move with the breath. Breathe out as you tilt and in as you arch. Keep the movement smooth and easy, and let your floor follow along.

A mobile pelvis makes everything easier, from lifting to loving. This is a move that pays you back for years.

Form checkSmall and smooth, with the breath leading. Your belly and jaw stay soft.
