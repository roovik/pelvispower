The Elevator

Lift in steps

The Elevator is a favorite for a reason. It trains control, and control is power. Instead of one big squeeze, you lift in small steps, one floor at a time, and then come down the same way.

Picture a tall building with five floors. Lift a little to reach the first floor, a little more for the second, and keep climbing. Pause at the top, then descend one floor at a time.

The way down is where the magic is. A slow descent asks the muscles to let go little by little, and that fine control makes you feel wonderfully in charge of your body. It is also the part that most people skip.

If you lose count of the floors, return to the ground and start again. A tidy trip beats a long one. Breathe out as you climb and breathe in as you descend.

Many men say this move feels like a dimmer switch on pleasure. Today you practice finding the stops along the dial.

Form checkSmall steps, smooth breathing, and a slow trip down. Stop at the floor you can control.
