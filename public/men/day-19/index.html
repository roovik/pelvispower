The Combo

Chain the moves into a routine

You know the moves. Today you string them into a sequence, like a circuit at the gym. A Combo is a short routine that flows from one move into the next without a pause.

Start with a few Salutes to wake things up. Slide into a Full Lift and hold for eight seconds. Climb the Elevator, come down slowly, and finish with a few Pulses and a long Soft Drop.

The flow is the point. Each move sets up the next, and your floor learns to change gears smoothly. Smooth gear changes are what performance uses all the time, from a slow build to a sudden spark.

Keep your breath moving and your belly soft. If the sequence gets sloppy, take a Soft Drop and begin again. Neat and calm beats fast and frantic.

Many men find the Combo feels like a conversation with their own body. It asks a question, listens to the answer and asks another.

Form checkNeat and calm beats fast and frantic. If the sequence slips, drop and start fresh.
