The Edge

Learn the rising wave

Staying power starts with knowing your own wave. Arousal rises, crests and falls, and a man who knows the shape of his wave can ride it. Today you bring a little arousal into the training so your floor can learn the real thing.

The Edge is a gentle practice. You lie back in thin clothes and let light pressure, a soft touch over the fabric, bring warmth. When the feeling is pleasant and rising, you pause the touch, lift and hold, and then let go with a Soft Drop and a long breath out. Then you let the feeling settle.

The secret is the release. Many men instinctively clench to slow down, and clenching tends to add tension. A slow breath and a soft floor bring the wave back down, and that is the skill you are practicing.

Keep everything light and easy. Stay at a pleasant level, around a 3 or 4 on the Dial, and stay there for a while. An orgasm is welcome if it arrives, and it is not today's goal.

If you feel tense or hurried, stop and breathe. There is no wrong way to do this. A calm Edge is a good Edge.
