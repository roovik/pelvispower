The Final Exam

Show off what you have built

Exam day, and it is a happy one. You take the same three tests you took on the first day, and your new numbers get to shine. Nobody is grading but you.

Warm up well and breathe. Take each test calmly, with a Soft Drop in between. Give every test your best effort and enjoy the result.

Here are the three tests again. Your longest hold, in seconds, stopping at 30. Your salutes in thirty seconds. And your steps up, stopping at 10.

Write the new numbers on the right side of the Before and After page. Notice the difference. Whatever it looks like, you have built something real, and many men are delighted by the gap.

Numbers are only part of the story. How your body feels, how you respond to touch and how much you know about your own pleasure all count too. They are the parts that do not fit in a box, and they are the best part.

Today's Set

Time
10 minutes.

Squeeze and release as fast as you can for half a minute. Count each one.
Test 3, Steps up
Lift a little, then a little more, as far as you can in separate steps. Count them and stop at 10.
 
Write all three numbers on the right side of the Before and After page.

A long Soft Drop and five slow breaths.

Variation
Take the tests with a friend or partner, and compare cheerfully.
