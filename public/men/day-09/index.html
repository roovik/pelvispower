The Bridge

Lift with your legs

A glute bridge is one of the best exercises there is, and it is even better with a pelvic floor lift at the top. The legs, the buttocks and the floor all work as a team. Today you add the floor to a classic.

Lie on your back with your knees bent and your feet flat. Press through your feet and raise your hips until your body forms a straight line from shoulders to knees. At the top, add a Full Lift. Then lower with a Soft Drop.

The bridge builds the big muscles around the pelvis. It also teaches your floor to work with them, so that lifting and loving feel like one skill. Strong glutes make a strong base for everything.

Keep your ribs down and your feet planted. Squeeze your buttocks without clenching your jaw. Rise on the breath out and lower on the breath in.

Plenty of men feel solid and powerful after a few bridges. Take that feeling with you into the rest of the day.

Form checkRibs down, feet planted, and the lift at the top. Lower with a full Soft Drop.
