Blood Flow

How strength keeps things firm

An erection is a blood-flow event. Blood rushes in and the muscles around the root squeeze to help keep it there. The muscle that wraps the base of your trunk is a quiet hero in this story.

A stronger floor can help it do that job. A few studies found that men with erection trouble who trained their floor saw real gains, and many improved a great deal. Think of it as a cheerful add-on to a doctor's advice.

Your lifestyle matters too. Good sleep, regular movement, less alcohol and no smoking all help blood flow. Your floor is one piece of a bigger picture.

Today you train the muscle that grips the root. Lift with a gentle Full Lift and feel the base of the trunk tighten, then release completely. Think of the lift as tightening a strap, and the drop as letting it go.

Strength and release are partners. A trained floor holds firm when it should and relaxes when it should, and both skills matter equally.

Form checkGentle lift, complete release. Keep the buttocks and belly soft.
