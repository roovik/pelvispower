Breathe Into It

The breath is the brake and the accelerator

Your breath and your floor are connected. When you breathe in, your floor drops gently. When you breathe out, it lifts. Learn to ride that rhythm and you gain a control panel for pleasure.

Slow, deep belly breaths bring the feeling down and spread it out. A held breath speeds things up and makes the muscles tense. Many men find that a long, slow exhale, with a soft sound on it, is the smoothest brake there is.

Today you add breath and sound to everything. Breathe in and drop. Breathe out with a soft "ahh" and lift. Let your jaw hang loose.

Sound is a friendly tool. A sigh, a hum or an "ahh" keeps the breath moving and makes the body feel safe enough to open. Use any sound you like, as quiet or as loud as the room allows.

Pair this with a little warmth if you like. Light pressure over the fabric, slow breath and a floor that follows the breath make a lovely trio.

Form checkLet the breath lead and the floor follow. Keep your jaw loose and your sounds relaxed.
