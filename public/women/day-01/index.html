Meet Your Floor

Find the muscles that run your pleasure

Your pelvic floor has worked without a day off for years. It has held you up, kept you dry and joined every orgasm you ever had. Today you meet it properly.

Finding it takes about a minute. Lie on your back with your knees bent. Imagine you are holding back a little gas. The soft lift you feel around your rosebud is part of your floor.

Now imagine you are stopping the flow of pee. The squeeze you feel at the front, around your pee hole and your kitty, is the other part. Try the stop-the-flow trick once, only to learn the feeling. Using it as a regular exercise can confuse your bladder, so the rest of the month happens away from the toilet.

The real feeling is a small, quiet lift, up and in, like drawing a drop of water upward. Your belly, thighs and buttocks stay soft. If your whole body clenches, you are working too hard, and a gentler squeeze is perfect.

Many women are surprised by how small the movement is. It looks like nothing from the outside, and that is why you can train anywhere. Tiny and precise is the target.
