Lift While You Move

Bring the floor into your day

A floor that only works lying down is a floor on holiday. Today you train it while you move, because real life happens on your feet. Walking, climbing stairs and carrying shopping are all opportunities.

Try a gentle lift on the exhale as you take a step. Try a lift as you rise from a chair, or as you pick something up from the floor. Each small lift is a free repetition.

This move trains timing. Your floor learns to switch on when your body works hard, and it does so without being told. That automatic response is what keeps you steady, dry and confident.

It also makes the floor feel more like part of you. Many women say this is when their pelvic floor starts to feel like an old friend. The connection carries over into pleasure, where body awareness is half the game.

Keep your breath moving and your belly soft. A gentle lift is plenty.

Form checkGentle is plenty. Breathe out, lift softly and carry on with your day.

Move 2, Lift and walk
Walk slowly for one minute, lifting gently on every second step.
Move 3, Chair lift
Sit and stand 10 times, lifting as you rise.

10 reps.

A Soft Drop.
