The Rock

Hips that move, a floor that follows

Your pelvic floor does not live alone. It rides on a bowl of bones that tilts forward, tilts back and circles like a belly dancer. When the bowl moves freely, the floor moves freely too.

Today you add movement. The Rock is a tilt of the pelvis, and it loosens the hips, the lower back and the muscles that hold tension without telling you. A free pelvis feels better in nearly everything, sex included.

On all fours, breathe in and let your back arch gently, with your tailbone lifting. Breathe out and tuck your tailbone under, like a cat curling. Let your floor drop on the inhale and lift on the exhale.

Then draw slow circles with your hips. Imagine your tailbone is a pencil and you are drawing a big, lazy circle on the ceiling. Slow wins, and small circles count.

Many women find the Rock lovely for the lower back. A happy back is a nice bonus, and a loose pelvis is a happy beginning.

Form checkMove slowly and keep your shoulders away from your ears. Small circles count.

On your period? Power through, go gentle, swap in a Recess or pause. It is your call. See the options.

Time

Position

Five slow breaths and a Soft Drop.

Move 3, The Full Lift
10 reps with 5-second holds, in any comfortable position.

A Soft Drop in Child's Pose.
