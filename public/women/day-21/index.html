Hold at the Edge

Tension and release, as the feeling rises

Arousal has two parts. One is the build, a slow gathering of warmth and attention. The other is the release, when the muscles let go and pleasure flows. Your floor can practice both.

Today you hold a gentle lift as the feeling rises, and you notice what it does. Many women find the lift sharpens the sensation, like a bright light over a small spot. When you release, the warmth spreads, and the contrast is part of the pleasure.

Try a slow rhythm. Build warmth with light pressure over the fabric, lift and hold for five seconds, then release for five. Repeat, and let each cycle go a little further.

Notice the moment of release. That moment is full of information, and many women find it is where the feeling opens. Following it with curiosity is the whole practice.

Orgasm is welcome and never required. If it arrives, enjoy it, and if it waits, enjoy the warmth. Either way your floor is learning.
