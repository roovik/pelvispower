Strong and soft, together

Recess again. Today the reading is about the difference between strong and tight. A strong floor can lift and can also release completely.

Some women have a floor that is too tight, and more squeezing makes it worse. Signs include pain with sex, a hard time relaxing and a constant feeling of tension down there. If this sounds like you, a pelvic floor physical therapist is a wonderful ally. They can help with release, and release is the point.

For everyone, this book includes the Soft Drop every day for that reason. A floor that drops well tends to enjoy being touched, and it lets pleasure in. Strength and softness work together.

Today give your floor a long, easy rest. Breathe slowly, let the muscles go loose and soften your belly, hips and jaw. Warmth helps, so a hand on your lower belly or a warm towel is lovely.

Then follow what feels good. Pleasure is today's reward, and you set its size. An orgasm is welcome and never required.

On your period? Power through, go gentle, swap in a Recess or pause. It is your call. See the options.
