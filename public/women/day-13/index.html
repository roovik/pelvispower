Flick, Then Hold

Quick and steady, in one breath

Real pleasure uses both kinds of muscle. The quick ones make the sparkle and the steady ones make the glow. Today you train them in a single sequence.

The move is simple. You flick quickly, ten times, then lift and hold for eight seconds, then drop. It feels like a tiny drumroll followed by a long, warm chord.

The switch from fast to slow is the skill. Many women notice that the hold feels fuller right after the flicks, as if the muscles have been warmed up and are listening. That sense of readiness is what you are building.

Keep your breath moving the whole time. A soft "ha" on the flicks and slow breathing on the hold work well. If the hold trembles, drop and start the sequence fresh.

Form checkCrisp flicks first, then a smooth hold. Keep your breath moving through both.

On your period? Power through, go gentle, swap in a Recess or pause. It is your call. See the options.

Today's Set

Time
7 minutes.
Position
Sitting tall, feet flat.
Garment
Your favorite yoga pants or a thin skirt.
Warm-up
Five slow breaths and a Soft Drop.
Move 1, Flick, Then Hold
10 quick flicks, then a Full Lift held for 8 seconds, then a Soft Drop for 10 seconds. Do 5 rounds.
Move 2, The Elevator
5 trips, 5 floors each way.
Cool-down
A Soft Drop and three slow breaths.
Pleasure Dial
3. Enjoy the contrast between sparkle and glow.
Variation
Do the sequence lying on your back.
The Log
One line. How long can you hold after the flicks?
